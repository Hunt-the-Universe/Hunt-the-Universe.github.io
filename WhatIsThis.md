# Overview

A free, self-guided scavenger hunt that's almost as weird as Fremont is.

Hunt the Universe is a neighborhood-inspired event based on [the University of Chicago Scavenger Hunt](https://scavhunt.uchicago.edu/) and [Middlebury's J-Term Hunt](https://sites.middlebury.edu/thehuntjterm/). 

In this event, five-to-ten person teams will race to respond to as many clues as possible. Some clues require a "correct" answer, others a creative interpretation. Some clues are worth a point, others worth many. Ultimately, the team with the most points will win our prize.
