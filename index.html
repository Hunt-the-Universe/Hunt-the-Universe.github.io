Welcome to Hunt the Universe
  • Instagram

A free all-day competitive scavenger hunt across the Fremont Neighborhood of Seattle.

Teams of up to ten will spend the day completing clues all over Fremont: solving puzzles, making absurd things, digging up neighborhood history, exploring nooks and crannies, giving back to the community, and more. Teams will have twelve hours to work on ~100 clues, after which the all-powerful Hunt Masters will award points and, if we do say so ourselves, some hella good prizes. This isn't your grandmother's scavenger hunt: think Taskmaster or the University of Chicago Scav with a twist of Fremont weird. Hunt the Universe is designed for adults, but it is suitable for all ages (more-or-less. Do you mind explaining to your kid what a polycule is?).
