Welcome to Hunt the Universe!

A free, self-guided scavenger hunt that’s almost as weird as Fremont is.

Hunt the Universe is a neighborhood-inspired event based on the University of Chicago Scavenger Hunt and Middlebury's J-Term Hunt. During the hunt, five-to-ten person teams will race to respond to as many clues as possible. Some clues require a “correct” answer, others a creative interpretation. Some clues are worth a point, others worth many. Ultimately, the team with the most points will win our prize.
